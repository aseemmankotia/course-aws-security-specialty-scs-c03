| 1 | SCS-C03 exam time limit and question count? | 170 minutes for 65 questions = 2.6 minutes per question average. Exam tip: Maintain 2-minute pace on easier questions to bank time for complex scenarios (4-5 minutes each). Flag questions taking >3 minutes. |
| 2 | SCS-C03 passing score on scaled 100-1000 scale? | 750 or higher (roughly 72% correct, but scaled scoring adjusts for question difficulty). Exam tip: You don't need perfection - strategic guessing and domain focus can get you to 750. Track domain scores to identify weak areas. |
| 3 | Distribution of question types in SCS-C03? | Multiple choice (1 correct): 70%, Multiple response (2-3 correct): 20%, Ordering: 5%, Matching: 5%. Exam tip: Multiple choice = fastest points. Flag complex multiple response for later review. |
| 4 | Multiple response elimination strategy when certain about 2 of 3 required answers? | Eliminate answers that are wrong CATEGORY (e.g., detective vs preventive controls). Remaining options have 33-50% guess probability vs 20% random. Exam tip: Never leave multiple response blank - partial credit improves domain scores. |
| 5 | When to flag a question during first pass through exam? | Flag if: (1) Taking >3 minutes, (2) Eliminated to 2 options but unsure, (3) Scenario unclear after one read, (4) Multiple response with only 1-2 known answers. Don't flag if you have 70%+ confidence. |
| 6 | IAM policy evaluation order for SCS-C03 ordering questions? | 1) Deny by default 2) SCPs (org boundaries) 3) Permissions boundaries (max permissions) 4) Identity-based policies 5) Session policies (temp restrictions) 6) Resource-based policies. Exam tip: SCPs always override, resource policies can override denies in steps 3-5. |
| 7 | Time allocation strategy with 20 questions and 45 minutes remaining? | Answer ALL unanswered questions first (preserve 2-minute pace), then review flagged questions prioritizing weakest domains. Exam tip: Unanswered = guaranteed 0 points. Flagged educated guess = possible points toward domain threshold. |
| 8 | How to identify correct answer in AWS service matching questions? | Apply rule: Specific service name → specific use case. Management/Orchestration service → broad/centralized use case. Example: Network Firewall (specific) → VPC traffic filtering. Firewall Manager (management) → centralized WAF rules across accounts. |
| 9 | Domain weight distribution in SCS-C03 (highest to lowest)? | Incident Response: 24-26%, Data Protection: 22-24%, Identity: 20-22%, Infrastructure: 18-20%, Management: 16-18%. Exam tip: Weak performance in Incident Response or Data Protection hurts scaled score most. Prioritize these domains if below 72%. |
| 10 | Best study strategy if domain scores are Identity 85%, Management 60%, Data Protection 68%? | Spend 70% study time on Management and Data Protection (both below 72% threshold), 30% maintaining Identity. Exam tip: Marginal improvement in weak, high-weight domains (Data Protection 22-24%) yields more scaled score gain than perfecting strong domains. |
| 11 | Scenario question keyword pairs that indicate conflicting requirements? | Common pairs: (1) 'least cost' + 'high availability', (2) 'prevent access' + 'break-glass emergency', (3) 'automatic' + 'approval required', (4) 'real-time' + 'least operational overhead'. Exam tip: Correct answer addresses BOTH constraints, wrong answers solve only one. |
| 12 | How to handle 15 minutes remaining with 10 flagged questions? | Triage by guess probability: Review 50/50 multiple choice first (6-8 min), then multiple response with 2/3 known (4-5 min), skip unclear scenarios unless time remains. Exam tip: Maximize expected value per minute - high-confidence revisions over low-confidence unknowns. |
